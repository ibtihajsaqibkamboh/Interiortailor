ABOUT INTERIOR TAILOR

About Interior Tailor

Interior Tailor is a practical online resource for people planning painting and home improvement projects.

Our goal is simple: make common painting calculations and color planning easier to understand and easier to perform.

Painting a room often starts with a few basic questions: How much paint do I need? How much of the wall area will actually be painted? How will a particular color look in a room? Can I explore a custom color before buying paint?

Interior Tailor brings these tasks together in one place.

Our Paint Calculator

Our Paint Calculator helps users estimate the amount of paint required for measurable painting surfaces. The calculator can take into account room dimensions, doors, windows, ceilings, number of coats, paint coverage and waste allowance.

The result is intended as a planning estimate rather than a guarantee of the exact amount required for a project. Paint consumption can vary depending on the product, surface condition, texture, application method and other real-world conditions.

Our Color Tools

Interior Tailor also provides tools for exploring paint colors. The Color Mixing Lab allows users to experiment with target HEX colors and estimated digital mixing recipes. The Room Color Visualizer provides a visual way to explore how selected colors may appear in a room environment.

These tools are designed to help users explore ideas before making a physical paint purchase.

Why We Built Interior Tailor

Painting projects can become unnecessarily complicated when calculations, measurements and color decisions are handled separately. We created Interior Tailor to bring some of these everyday planning tasks together into simple browser-based tools.

The tools are designed to be straightforward, accessible and useful for homeowners, renters, DIY enthusiasts and anyone planning a painting project.

Important Information About Our Estimates

Paint estimates and color visualizations are provided for planning and informational purposes. Actual paint requirements may differ from calculator results because of surface condition, paint formulation, coverage rate, application technique and other project-specific factors.

Likewise, digital color previews cannot guarantee how physical paint will look on a particular wall. Always check the manufacturer's product information and, when appropriate, test a physical paint sample before starting a large painting project.
