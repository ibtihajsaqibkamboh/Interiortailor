ABOUT PAINTTOOLS

Simple tools for planning your next paint project

PaintTools brings practical calculators and color exploration features together in one easy-to-use website.

What we do

Our tools are designed to help people estimate paint quantities for rooms and explore custom paint colors before starting a project. The goal is to make common painting calculations easier to understand and quicker to work through.

Our tools

The Paint Calculator estimates the amount of paint needed from room dimensions, openings, coats, coverage and waste allowance. The Color Mixing Lab lets you explore a target color and an estimated digital mixing recipe, along with a room preview showing how the selected color may look on a wall.

About the estimates

Results are estimates intended for planning and experimentation. Actual paint usage and color appearance can vary because of surface texture, primer, application method, lighting, paint brand, pigment strength and product-specific coverage or tinting limits. Always check the information supplied by your paint manufacturer before purchasing or mixing paint.

Our approach

We aim to keep the tools straightforward, transparent and useful for everyday home projects. We do not present digital color recipes as manufacturer-verified formulas.
