PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains how Interior Tailor handles information when you use our calculators, color tools, articles and contact email.

Information you provide

If you email interiortailor@gmail.com, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and message. We use that information to respond to your request and address feedback. Messages are handled through our email provider, Google Gmail, and may be retained as needed to manage the conversation and relevant records. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

Calculators and browser storage

Our calculators and color tools perform their calculations in your browser. You do not need an account to use them. The paint calculator and color mixer use local storage to remember settings, and the site stores your analytics preference. You can remove saved settings using your browser's site-data controls. Clearing site data also clears your cookie preference.

Hosting and technical information

When you request a page, your browser sends technical information such as your IP address, browser details and requested URL to the server delivering the site. Hosting providers may process technical logs to deliver pages, maintain security and investigate errors.

Optional Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics only after you choose “Allow analytics” in our cookie preferences. It can collect usage and device information, including pages visited and interactions, and use cookies to distinguish visits. If you choose “Essential only,” the site does not load Google Analytics. You can change your choice using “Cookie preferences” in the footer. Withdrawal stops future analytics collection through this site and attempts to remove its analytics cookies; it does not erase data already processed by Google.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Google also provides an Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising and Google advertising cookies

Google AdSense advertising is not currently enabled in this version of the site. If we enable it, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on your previous visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies allow Google and its partners to personalize ads using visits across this and other sites on the Internet, subject to your choices and applicable consent requirements.

You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center. You may also be able to opt out of participating vendors' personalized advertising at the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page. We will update this policy to identify additional advertising vendors and networks if they are enabled. The site's current analytics preference controls Google Analytics; it is not an advertising consent platform.

External links

Our articles link to paint manufacturers and other resources. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Following an external link takes you to the service named in that link.

Privacy requests and retention

To ask about personal information you have shared with us, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, email interiortailor@gmail.com. We will consider your request in light of applicable requirements and any records we need to keep. Browser storage can be deleted directly in your browser; information held by third-party services is also subject to their own policies and retention settings.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when our data practices change. You can also use our Contact page for questions about this policy.
